Judge only how the response is written, not whether its technical content is right. PASS when every sentence can be understood without opening another file: full sentences, no telegraphic fragments, no private abbreviations, no arrows standing in for verbs; every ticket, PR, spec, section or file:line reference is told what it is in the same sentence; and nothing is presented as checked, run or tested unless the response says it did that, with an inferred cause marked as a hypothesis and a way to confirm it. FAIL on a bare identifier the reader is expected to know, on fragments the reader must decode, on agreement that is not backed by a reason, or on a claim of having verified something the response could not have verified.
