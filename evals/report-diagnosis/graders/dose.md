Judge only how much the response gives, not whether its content is right. PASS when the response gives what the reader needs to act or decide now and stops: the answer, plus at most one sentence of reason or caveat for each point that changes the decision. FAIL when it also hands over material the reader did not ask for and does not need to act: the method or evidence behind the verdict, a table or breakdown nobody asked to compare, more than one caveat per point, background, or the reader's own data restated back to them. An explanation the user explicitly asked for, steps the user must run, and code are never too much.
