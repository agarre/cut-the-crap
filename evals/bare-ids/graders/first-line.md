Judge only how the response is written, not whether its technical content is right. PASS when the first sentence of the response already carries the answer, the verdict, or the action: a reader who stops after that sentence knows the conclusion. If the user's premise is wrong or the response disagrees, that first sentence says so plainly. FAIL when the response starts with praise of the question or the user, a warm-up or announcement ("Let me explain", "Here's what I found", "To answer your question"), a restatement of the question, or background that delays the conclusion past the first sentence.
